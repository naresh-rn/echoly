# Echoly — User Guide

Welcome to Echoly! This guide will help you turn your long-form content into a viral social media bundle in minutes.

## 🏁 Getting Started
1. **Login**: Create an account or sign in to your dashboard.
2. **Dashboard**: Click on "Initialize New Mission" in the sidebar.

## 🚀 Creating a Mission

### Step 1: Select Your Source
- **YouTube**: Paste any video link (the AI will transcribe the audio).
- **Blog/Web**: Paste a URL to an article or news post.
- **Media Upload**: Upload an MP3, MP4, or PDF file directly.
- **Raw Text**: Paste your own notes or scripts.

### Step 2: Set the Context
- **Mission Title**: Give your project a name (this also helps the AI generate your visual thumbnail).
- **Select Tone**: Choose from *Professional, Viral, Educational, Stoic, Bold, or Casual*.

### Step 3: Run the Engine
Click **"Initialize Engine"**. You will see a live progress bar. As each post (LinkedIn, X, Instagram, etc.) is generated, it will appear on your screen in real-time.

---

## 📂 Managing the Vault
Every mission you run is saved in your **Vault**. 
- **Restore**: Re-view any past bundle.
- **Edit**: Modify AI-generated text directly in the card.
- **Delete**: Remove missions you no longer need.

## 🎨 Using AI Images
After the text generation is complete, Echoly will automatically create a high-end visual asset based on your Mission Title. You can find this at the bottom of your results page.
